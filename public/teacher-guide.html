Start of year setup

Do these four in order, once.

  1. Make your account. Open the tracker, choose Teacher Access, then Need an account? Register here. Enter your last name exactly as it appears in the roster's homeroom column, pick your role, and set a password.
  2. Check your roster. Homeroom teachers land on My Class Roster. If it's empty, your last name doesn't match the roster — ask an admin to fix it, or use Add Student for a late arrival.
  3. Set your class goal. On the My Class Ticket Goal card, click the shield icon, enter a ticket target and the reward, then Save Goal. Students see the progress bar too.
  4. Hand out logins. Print Login Cards → Print Now gives you a cut-apart card per student with their number and PIN.

Every day

All of this lives on the student cards.

Give a ticket
Tap Respectful, Responsible, or Determined on the student's card. It saves the moment you tap.
Backdate it
Set Award Date to Today, Yesterday, or a specific date before you tap.
Attendance
Tap the Present chip to flip a student to Absent. Absent students can't be awarded or spend, and whole-class awards skip them.
Golden Ticket
The gold banner awards your whole class at once and counts toward your grade level's goal.
Class store
Spend under a balance → enter the number of tickets and what they bought → Confirm Spend.
Another class
Search Any Student at the bottom finds anyone in the school, then you pick a reason.
Undo
Delete a mistake from the Recent Tickets list at the bottom of your dashboard.
Specialists: pick a class first, then use Award Whole Class to give every present student one ticket.

Reading a student card

  • The green circle is the spendable balance: everything earned school-wide, minus everything spent.
  • The R / S / D chips count only the tickets you gave — another teacher's tickets won't show there.
  • Goal progress counts lifetime tickets earned, so spending at the store never sets your class back.
  • The gear icon opens a student's login number, PIN, homeroom, and grade — that's where you fix a forgotten PIN.

Display Mode & the raffle

  • Display Mode fills the screen with your roster for the projector. Tap a name, pick a value, done. Hide Balances swaps every number for a “?” when you'd rather the class not compare. Exit returns to your dashboard.
  • Weekly Raffle lives in the top bar. Choose students or teachers, a window of one week, one month, or all time, narrow to a grade or homeroom if you like, then draw. Every ticket earned is one entry, so busy earners have better odds.

What students see

Student Portal tab · student number + 4-digit PIN

  • Their spendable balance and the full history of what they earned and spent.
  • Progress bars for your class goal and their grade's Golden Ticket goal.
  • A mascot avatar they pick, and a timed math game for early finishers.

If something looks wrong

Empty roster
Your registered last name doesn't match the homeroom name on the roster. An admin can correct either one.
Wrong student
Remove the ticket in Recent Tickets, then award the right student.
Spend is greyed out
The balance is zero, or the student is marked absent.
Missing tools
Roster imports, merging duplicate students, and grade goals are admin-only — switch with Role (needs the admin password).
